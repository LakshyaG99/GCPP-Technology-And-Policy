THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

About Me

Lakshya Gupta

SOFTWARE ENGINEER · AI, TECHNOLOGY & PUBLIC POLICY

I am a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from IIT (BHU) Varanasi, with over five years of professional experience in software engineering, financial technology, and applied artificial intelligence at JPMorgan Chase & Co.

My interests extend beyond the technical development of emerging technologies to their broader implications for society, governance, and public institutions. I am particularly interested in AI governance, technology policy, and the evolving relationship between technological innovation and human agency.

Through Takshashila Institution's Graduate Certificate in Public Policy, I have been exploring how technical expertise and interdisciplinary policy analysis can inform the responsible development, adoption, and governance of technology.
